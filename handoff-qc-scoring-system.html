Handoff: Evaluation & Design of ONT QC Preprocessing Scoring System and Dashboard

1. Context & Research Problem

  • Project: BIOX7021 Master of Bioinformatics Final Project (Oxford Nanopore Technologies / ONT Quality Control Preprocessing Benchmarking).
  • Core Phenomenon: Empirical benchmarking across 13 bacterial reference genomes (Hall et al. 2024 dataset) across basecalling models (hac, sup) and depths (20x, 50x, 100x) reveals that no single QC pipeline wins across all assembly dimensions:
    • Filtlong combinations maximize sequence accuracy (minimizing indels/mismatches) but discard reads from small, low-copy plasmids.
    • Raw / Untrimmed datasets retain 100% of small plasmids and chromosomal contiguity, but carry residual adapter/barcode contamination and higher base errors.
    • Chopper / Dorado provides a balanced compromise.
  • Goal for Next Session: Rigorously evaluate, critique, and refine the multi-criteria scoring system and interactive Quarto Shinylive dashboard (tool_weighting.qmd), verifying whether current mathematical assumptions are biologically and statistically appropriate.

3. Current Mathematical Architecture & Implementation Details

A. Sub-Score Normalisation (0 to 100 scale)

  1. Structural Contiguity (\(S_{\text{aungar}}\)): Symmetrical linear deviation from the ideal \(1.0\) reference ratio: \[S_{\text{aungar}} = \max(0, 1 - |x - 1|) \times 100\]
  2. Sequence Error (\(S_{\text{err}}\)): Inverted min-max scaling of Mismatches and Indels per 100 kbp, pooled equally: \[S_{\text{mismatches}} = \frac{\max(M) - x_M}{\max(M) - \min(M)} \times 100, \quad S_{\text{indels}} = \frac{\max(I) - x_I}{\max(I) - \min(I)} \times 100\] \[S_{\text{err}} = \frac{S_{\text{mismatches}} + S_{\text{indels}}}{2}\]
  3. Contamination (\(S_{\text{cont}}\)) & Missed Contigs (\(S_{\text{miss}}\)): Inverted min-max scaling: \[S = \frac{\max(x) - x}{\max(x) - \min(x)} \times 100\]

B. Aggregation Formulations (Toggleable in UI)

  • Option 1 (Default & Recommended) — Shifted Zero-Safe Geometric Mean: \[\text{Final Score} = \left( (S_{\text{aungar}} + 1)^{w_n} \times (S_{\text{err}} + 1)^{w_e} \times (S_{\text{cont}} + 1)^{w_c} \times (S_{\text{miss}} + 1)^{w_m} \right)^{\frac{1}{\sum w}} - 1\]
  • Option 2 — Original Geometric Mean with \(\epsilon = 10^{-5}\) Floor: \[\text{Final Score} = \left( \prod S_{i, \text{safe}}^{w_i} \right)^{\frac{1}{\sum w}}, \quad \text{where } S_{\text{safe}} = \max(S, 10^{-5})\]
  • Default Survey Weights: Informed by the Microbial QC Survey: \[w_{\text{miss}} = 0.35, \quad w_{\text{err}} = 0.28, \quad w_{\text{aungar}} = 0.20, \quad w_{\text{cont}} = 0.17\]

C. WebAssembly / Performance Design

  • The app runs in-browser via Pyodide (shinylive-python).
  • Dataset loading is cached in @reactive.Calc def load_metrics_data() to prevent redundant HTTP requests when sliders move.

4. Specific Assumptions to Critically Evaluate

The incoming agent should assess the validity and potential refinements of the following assumptions:

  1. Min-Max Scaling vs. Absolute Biological Thresholds (Noise Magnification & Rank Reversal):
    • Issue: Min-max bounds change dynamically when filtering by depth (20x vs 100x). At 100x depth, mismatch rates are tiny across all tools (e.g. 0.010 vs 0.012/100kb), yet min-max assigns a 100-point penalty for a 0.002 biological difference.
    • Question: Should we introduce absolute sigmoid decay curves (\(S = 100 / (1 + e^{(x-\theta)/\sigma})\)) or keep min-max for relative benchmarking?
  2. Symmetric auNGA Penalty (\(|x - 1|\)):
    • Issue: An auNGA of \(0.6\) (severe chromosomal fragmentation) is penalized equally to \(1.4\) (minor repeat duplication / end overlap). In assembly practice, fragmentation is far worse.
    • Question: Should an asymmetric piecewise function (e.g. harsher penalty for \(x < 1.0\)) be adopted?
  3. Indel vs Mismatch 50/50 Pooling in \(S_{\text{err}}\):
    • Issue: Nanopore R10.4.1 errors are mostly homopolymer indels that cause CDS frameshifts, whereas substitution mismatches are rare.
    • Question: Should separate sliders/weights be exposed for indels vs mismatches?
  4. Shift Parameter Magnitude (\(+1\) offset on \(0-100\) scale):
    • Issue: The \(+1\) offset on a scale of \(100\) means \(S \in [1, 101]\). If one score is 0 and three are 100 (equal weights), the score is \((101^3 \times 1)^{1/4} - 1 \approx 30.8\).
    • Question: Is this non-compensatory penalty level well-calibrated, or should the shift be scaled relative to the domain (e.g., scoring on \([0, 1]\) with offset \(+c\))?
  5. Loss of Sample Variance in Group Means:
    • Issue: df.groupby().mean() calculates an average across all 13 bacterial species. Outliers (e.g. low GC vs high GC genomes) are obscured.
    • Question: How should within-cohort rank variance or error bars be visualized in the Shinylive dashboard?
